# Store Listing Draft

App name:

WA Schedule

Short description:

Schedule your own WhatsApp messages on your device.

Full description:

WA Schedule helps you create local rules for sending your own WhatsApp messages at selected days, specific dates, or date/time pairs.

Create a rule, choose a WhatsApp chat or group name, write the message, select the schedule, and run a dry check or test send before relying on scheduled execution. Rules and history stay on your device.

WA Schedule uses Android Accessibility permission to operate WhatsApp only for rules you create. It verifies the selected target, fills the message field, taps send, and returns to Home. The app does not provide a cloud account, does not include ads, and does not use analytics SDKs.

Important notes:

- WhatsApp must be installed and configured separately.
- WA Schedule is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.
- Automated sends depend on Android permissions, device state, and WhatsApp UI availability.
- Do not use this app for spam or unsolicited messaging.

Feature bullets:

- Weekly, specific-date, and multiple date/time pair scheduling.
- Dry Run and Test Send checks.
- Local execution history.
- English, Ukrainian, and Russian UI.
