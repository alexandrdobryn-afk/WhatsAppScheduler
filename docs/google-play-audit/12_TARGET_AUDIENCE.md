# Target Audience

Recommended target audience:

- Adults
- General productivity users who personally configure scheduled WhatsApp messages

Not recommended:

- Children under 13
- Families program
- Child-directed positioning

Reasons:

- App interacts with WhatsApp, which has its own age and account requirements.
- App stores user-authored message text and chat/group target names locally.
- App uses AccessibilityService and exact scheduled automation.

Recommended Play Console position:

- Target age: 18+
- Not designed for children
- Not participating in Families

Store listing copy should avoid:

- Claims aimed at children.
- Bulk spam, mass marketing, scraping, growth-hacking, or unsolicited messaging wording.
- Claims that imply WhatsApp endorsement.
