WA Schedule

Scheduled WhatsApp messages for Android.

WA Schedule is a local Android application for user-configured deterministic schedules on a personal WhatsApp account.

  • Runs locally on Android.
  • Uses user-configured schedule rules.
  • Works with a personal WhatsApp account.
  • Does not operate a backend server.
  • Does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.
